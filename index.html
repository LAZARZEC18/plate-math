      Questions

      How does it choose the plates?
      It takes as many of the heaviest plate as fit, limited by how many pairs you own, then works down the sizes. If that misses, it checks every combination you own for an exact match. If none exists, it shows the nearest weights you can load below and above.
      Why does the step change from 2.5 kg?
      The minus and plus buttons move by one pair of your smallest plate. With 1.25 kg plates that's 2.5 kg. Add 0.5 kg fractional plates under My plates and the step drops to 1 kg.
      Which 1RM estimate should I trust?
      None is exact. Plate Math averages Epley, Brzycki and Lombardi, which agree closely between 2 and 10 reps. For heavy top sets, the RPE method is usually closer, because it accounts for how many reps you had left.
      What is a training max in 5/3/1?
      A slightly reduced 1RM, usually 85 to 90% of your true max. Every 5/3/1 percentage is taken from it, which keeps the work submaximal so you can keep adding weight for months. Week 4 is a lighter deload.
      Do collars count toward the weight?
      Turn on Collars if you use competition collars, which weigh 2.5 kg a pair (5 lb in pounds mode). Leave it off for spring clips, which weigh next to nothing.
      Where is my data stored?
      Only on this device, in your browser's storage. Nothing is uploaded. Use Export CSV in the Log to keep a copy or move it to a spreadsheet.
